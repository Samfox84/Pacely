The first public version of Pacely: a calm, private day planner for ADHD, autistic and AuDHD people. Free and open source, with no accounts, ads or tracking.

## Get it

| | |
|---|---|
| 💻 **Mac** | Download **Pacely-Mac.zip** below, unzip it, and drag Pacely into Applications. The first time, right-click › Open. [Mac guide](https://github.com/Samfox84/Pacely/blob/main/mac/README.md) |
| 📱 **iPhone and Apple Watch** | Build it with Xcode on a Mac: use it on just your phone, or with your own server. [iPhone guide](https://github.com/Samfox84/Pacely/blob/main/ios/README.md) |
| 🏠 **Your own server** | An old laptop, Raspberry Pi or home server. [Server guide](https://github.com/Samfox84/Pacely/blob/main/docs/setup-server.md) |
| 🌐 **Browser** | [samfox84.github.io/Pacely](https://samfox84.github.io/Pacely/) |

New to it? Start with [How to use Pacely](https://github.com/Samfox84/Pacely/blob/main/docs/using-pacely.md).

## Calm by design
- **Calm layout**, **Low-stimulation mode** and **Quiet hours**: less on screen, nothing moving, no notifications when you choose.
- No ticking clocks or streaks. Reminders state things plainly ("Starting about now.") instead of giving orders.
- Easy-read fonts (Atkinson Hyperlegible, OpenDyslexic).

## Highlights
- Visual timeline, plain-words quick add, steps, routines, to-do, focus timer, "running late" and "couldn't do it today".
- Office and work-from-home days, calendars tidied to your working hours.
- *Make a list* turns a long message into a tidy checklist.
- With your own server: a private family helper, live sync, when-to-leave journeys (with a calm TfL check on office days), and an optional AI helper (private on your server, or your own Gemini key).

Pacely is still early (0.x). Please [tell us](https://github.com/Samfox84/Pacely/issues) what works and what feels like too much.
