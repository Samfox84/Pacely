# How to use Pacely

A short tour. You don't need to use everything: start with **Today**, and add more only if it helps.

## A calm start

Go to **Me** and try these. Each one takes something away rather than adding more:

- **Calm layout**: Today shows one line for the day, what's happening now, and your timeline. Everything else is a tap away.
- **Low-stimulation mode**: no sounds, no taps, nothing moves, softer colours.
- **Quiet hours**: no notifications at the times you choose (for example 8pm to 7am).
- **Text style**: *Easy read* (Atkinson Hyperlegible) or *OpenDyslexic*.

Turn off anything you don't use. Pacely is meant to take things off your mind, not add to it.

## Today

- The **timeline** shows your day as colour blocks. Bigger block, longer activity. Gaps show free time.
- **Now** shows what's happening, and when it ends ("until 9:15").
- Tap an activity to see its steps and notes. Tap the circle to tick it off.
- **Swipe left or right** anywhere to move between days.
- Running late? **Running late** moves the rest of today back.
- Couldn't do something? **Couldn't do it today** finds it a new day. No guilt, no streaks to break.

## Adding things

Type into **Add…** in plain words, and Pacely works out the time, day, emoji and colour:

| You type | You get |
|---|---|
| `dentist tomorrow 9:30` | Dentist, tomorrow at 9:30 |
| `call the bank fri at 2` | Call the bank, Friday at 2pm |
| `drop off kids every weekday 8:45` | Repeats Monday to Friday |
| `in the office every tue and thu` | An all-day office banner every Tuesday and Thursday |
| `wfh every mon` | Working from home every Monday |
| `day off friday` · `sick day today` | A quiet all-day day (reminders stay quiet) |

Press **+** for the full editor: steps, reminders, repeats, private, place and website.

## Steps, routines and focus

- **Steps** turn a big thing into small ones. The AI helper can suggest them if you've set it up.
- **Routines** (morning, start work, wind down, or your own) add a whole set in one tap.
- **Focus** runs a gentle timer through your steps. Nothing ticks loudly or flashes.

## To-do

Things without a time live in **To-do**, sorted into **Now**, **Soon** and **Later**. Drag one into your day when
you're ready. The 📋 button turns a long message (like a shopping list someone sent you) into a clear list you can
tick off: one thing per line, amounts first, shopping in the order you walk round a supermarket.

## Reminders

- Reminders say what the thing is, then a plain statement: "Starting about now.", "In 10 minutes, at 10:00."
- **Keep nudging me** (on an activity) gives up to three gentle follow-ups for things that really matter.
- **Gentle nudges** (Me) remind you to drink, eat and take a pause. Each one is a separate switch.
- On iPhone, press and hold a reminder for **Done**, **Running late** or **Remind me in 10 min**.

## Calendars

**Me › Calendars** shows Google, iCloud or Outlook calendars on your timeline. Tap one to **tidy** it: only your
working hours, no repeating blocks (focus time, out of office), and no copies of things already in Pacely.

## With your own server

- **Family helper**: **Me › Share with family** makes a private, one-time invite for a partner or family member.
  They open it in the Pacely app and can add things to your plan, send you lists, and see what you choose to share.
  Making a new invite signs the old phone out. Anything you mark **Private** never shows to them.
- **Getting about**: add your home postcode, and Pacely can work out when to leave for anything with a place.
  On office days in London it can watch your TfL route (tube, bus, trains: you choose) and tell you, once and
  calmly, if a delay changes when you need to leave. Reminders to leave are one gentle heads-up by default.
- **AI helper**: brain dump to tasks, talk it through, photo to tasks, and **✨ Find it and plan it**, which looks
  an event up online (website, price, address) and works out the journey. See the
  [server guide](setup-server.md#the-ai-helper-optional) to switch it on.

## Backups

**Me › Save a copy** makes one file with all your plans. **Load a file** lets you merge it or replace everything.
Your Gemini key is never in the file.
